Guild Wars 2 · Deimos · Blish HUD

Who Touched Oils

After every Deimos pull, see who stepped in Rapid Decay — and who touched each oil first.

Download module Source on GitHub

What it shows

Built for the wipe review, not the mid-fight shout.

  • First touch Who expanded each oil puddle first.
  • Hit totals How many times each player stood in oil.
  • Fair counts Downed and dead players are ignored.

Install in a minute

  1. Have Blish HUD and ArcDPS logging.
  2. Grab the latest .bhm from Releases.
  3. Drop it in your Blish modules folder and enable Deimos Oil Summary.
  4. Finish a Deimos pull — the panel appears when the log writes.

No ArcDPS ↔ Blish bridge required. It reads combat logs on disk.
